# Review of gene and allele evidence

Public phenotype vocabularies, disease catalogues and a skeletal disorder panel were compared with one another. The review keeps the strength of a published disease association separate from the clinical assertions for a specific allele. Sequence database matches require the same reference assembly, position, reference allele and alternate allele. A missing database entry is recorded separately from a measured allele frequency of zero.

Results were checked against primary publications where available. Experimental findings, human syndromes and group-level associations are labelled separately. This note describes the method only.
